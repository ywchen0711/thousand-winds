# Credits

## Monster sprites

Most ordinary monsters use sprites by these artists, licensed under [CC-BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/):

- **Charles Gabriel (Antifarea)**, "10 Fantasy RPG Enemies", hosted by OpenGameArt.org: https://opengameart.org/content/10-fantasy-rpg-enemies
  - Used here: the slimes, cockatrice, rat, wolves, scorpion and ghost.
- **Stephen "Redshrike" Challener**, hosted by OpenGameArt.org:
  - "10 Basic RPG Enemies", https://opengameart.org/content/10-basic-rpg-enemies (the wasp, tortoise, skeleton and serpent);
  - "More RPG Enemies", https://opengameart.org/content/more-rpg-enemies (the mushroom man, the merrow and the gargoyle).

**Changes made:** the sprites were cut out of their sheets, their backgrounds were made transparent, and they were packed into `assets/monsters.png`. In the game, stronger monsters of the same family are recoloured versions of the same sprite, bosses are drawn at double size, and the small dungeon versions are scaled down.

The bosses, the sand golem and the yeti are hand-drawn for this game (`js/pixmon.js`).
